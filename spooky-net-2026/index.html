Receive-only welcome

Everything on this net can be copied with a receiver, a computer, and free software. Here's how to follow along:

  1. Install Fldigi and Flamp (free, from W1HKJ). Both run on Windows, Mac, Linux and Raspberry Pi.
  2. Connect your receiver's audio to the computer. A cable to the sound card works, and so does a receiver with a built-in USB sound card.
  3. Tune to 7.121 MHz USB. In Fldigi, choose MFSK32, then click the waterfall at 1000 Hz.
  4. Turn RxID on (the RxID button in Fldigi). Fldigi will follow the net if it changes modes.
  5. Open Flamp. The bulletin file builds itself block by block. The pumpkin picture opens in Fldigi's picture window. Save it.

Want to try it before Friday? The BDN Net meets on the same frequency every Friday at the same time.

Haunted Bulletin

Tonight's bulletin goes out by Flamp like any Friday, and the relays carry it across the map. But tricks are hiding in it. Copy it, read it, and find them. Send your list to NCS by traffic, or reply to the announcement post. The answers come out the following Friday.

Trick or Traffic

Say TRICK or TREAT at check-in. A treat is a small file sent just to you. A trick is a task card you have to finish before the net closes. Gamble wisely.

Ghost Whisperer

"Summoning spirits from the ether." The volunteer stations are lined up ahead of time. Each one steps down from 100 W to 25, 5, and 1 W, sending a short beacon at each step. Everyone else notes the lowest step they could still decode. The station heard at the lowest power over the longest distance takes the title.

The Crypt

Something is locked in here. A trick, then a treat.

The way in arrives on the air on Friday night. Find out how to get it out in How to open the Crypt below. Until then, the Crypt stays sealed.

How to open the Crypt

The trick

The word that opens the Crypt is in the Great Pumpkin, the picture sent at the end of the net. It's written right there in plain sight. But it isn't easy to see.

Getting it out

  1. Copy the pumpkin picture in Fldigi and save it.
  2. Open it in any image viewer or editor, like GIMP, Paint, Photos or Preview.
  3. Zoom in and look closely at the pumpkin.
  4. Still can't find it? Turn the brightness down and the contrast up. Some things only show in the dark. Or lean back from the screen and squint.
  5. Type the word into the Crypt. Capital letters don't matter.

Missed the picture? Ask someone who copied it to relay it to you on the air. That's what nets are for.
