Can't find the word?

  1. Open your saved pumpkin picture in any image viewer or editor, like GIMP, Paint, Photos or Preview.
  2. Zoom in and look closely at the pumpkin.
  3. Still can't see it? Turn the brightness down and the contrast up. Some things only show in the dark. Or lean back from the screen and squint.
  4. Capital letters and spaces don't matter.

Missed the picture? Ask someone who copied it to relay it to you on the air. That's what nets are for.
